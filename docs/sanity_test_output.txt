TEST QUESTION: How do I add a YouTube video?
============================================================
To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* **Access Apps:** Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* **Select YouTube:** Search for "YouTube" and click on the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter Video Details:** Paste your YouTube video link (regular, share, or Shorts link) into the **URL** field, and optionally give it a **Name**.
* **Configure Options (Optional):** Adjust **Display options** (such as Mute or Captions) and **Playback options** (to set specific Start/End times).
* **Save & Assign:** Click **Save** to add the video to your Files/Assets, then assign it to your screens, playlists, or schedules.
