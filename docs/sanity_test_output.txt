TEST QUESTION: How do I add a Youtube video?
============================================================
To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* **Log in and open Apps:** Go to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, click **Apps**, and search for and select **YouTube**.
* **Enter video details:** Give your video a optional **Name** and paste your video's link into the **URL** field (supports regular video links, share links, or YouTube Shorts).
* **Configure options:** Optionally adjust display settings like Captions or Mute, or use Playback options to select a specific segment using "Start At" and "End At".
* **Save your asset:** Click **Save** to add the video to your Files/Assets.
* **Assign to screens:** Assign your newly created YouTube asset directly to your screens, playlists, or schedules.

For more details, check the full guide here: [Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051014713-How-to-Use-YouTube-with-OptiSigns](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051014713-How-to-Use-YouTube-with-OptiSigns)
